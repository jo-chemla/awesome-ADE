Nimbalyst pairs parallel agent sessions with visual WYSIWYG editors (markdown, mockups, Mermaid, Excalidraw, CSV, data models, Monaco) and red/green diff review of the agent's edits, plus task trackers the agents read and update. Everything is plain files in your repo.
