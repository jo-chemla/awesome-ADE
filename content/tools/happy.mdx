Happy started as a CLI wrapper (`happy claude`, `happy codex`) that mirrors a terminal session to a phone, and has grown into Happy Harness: a multi-provider desktop app reusing your Claude/Codex/Grok subscriptions, with mobile continuity and multiplayer sessions. The star count here is for the mobile/CLI/relay repo.
