AionUi positions itself as a cross-platform, multi-model alternative to Claude Cowork: a built-in agent (30+ model platforms) plus a Multi-Agent mode that drives Claude Code, Codex and other CLI agents through ACP. Strong on remote/chat-bot access and 24/7 scheduled automation; weaker on git-worktree isolation than most entries here.
